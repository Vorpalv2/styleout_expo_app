Data Deletion Instructions

To delete your Styleout data, sign in to the app and remove your profile photo, wardrobe items, saved looks, and connected Instagram account from the relevant settings. To request complete account deletion, contact the Styleout team using the email associated with your account and include the email address used to sign in.

We will verify the request and delete the associated profile, wardrobe images, saved looks, generated images, and Instagram connection data, except where retention is required by law.
