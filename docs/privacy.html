Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Styleout lets you organize wardrobe items, save looks, and generate outfit images. We collect account identifiers, profile photos, wardrobe images, saved looks, and information you enter about clothing items to provide these features.

How we use information

We use this information to display your wardrobe, create saved looks, process requested image generation, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and sharing

Your images and wardrobe data are stored using Supabase infrastructure. If you connect Instagram, Styleout requests only the permissions needed to display media you choose to import. Imported media is copied to your Styleout storage for use in the app.

Your choices

You can remove wardrobe items, photos, saved looks, or disconnect Instagram from inside the app. You may request deletion of your account data using the instructions on our data deletion page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Styleout team through the email associated with this app.
